TRISOCMD marketplace policy

These rules apply to every marketplace listing and conversation. Read them before contacting another user. This preview is not an active checkout or escrow platform.

  1. Legal ownership and transfer rights. Sellers may list only items they own and are permitted to transfer under relevant rules. Stolen, compromised or unauthorized accounts are forbidden.
  2. Accurate descriptions and real photos. Include the platform, condition, limitations and at least one genuine photo. Misleading listings, stock photos presented as the item, fake reviews and impersonation are prohibited.
  3. No fraud or manipulation. No scams, chargeback abuse, fake payment confirmations, pressure tactics, phishing links or requests for private recovery information.
  4. No secrets in messages. Never send passwords, session tokens, one-time codes, recovery phrases, private identification documents or other sensitive credentials.
  5. Verify independently. Approval is a moderation decision, not a guarantee of sale, account authenticity, seller reliability or compliance with a game's terms of service.
  6. Centralized payments (planned). Sellers must not request direct payment for marketplace-managed transactions. When a compliant payment process becomes active, buyers will pay TRISOCMD through official published payment instructions; TRISOCMD would confirm cleared funds, verify delivery and any dispute, then arrange seller payout less any disclosed marketplace fee. This is a planned process, not a live escrow guarantee.
  7. Current payment status. Purchase requests only ask the administrator to review an offer. The site does not currently accept deposits, hold funds, verify crypto transactions, release seller payouts or guarantee refunds. Never send money based on a private chat, screenshots or unverified payment details.
  8. Seller's 4% fee. Any future supported marketplace transaction may include a 4% service commission, clearly displayed before payment. No fee is currently charged through this preview.
  9. Disputes and delivery. If a payment workflow is activated, sellers must provide the described product and cooperate with buyer and moderator verification. Neither party may pressure the other to mark a delivery complete prematurely. Unresolved complaints are reviewed before any seller payout.
  10. Listing removal. Sellers can withdraw their own listings, and administrators can remove any listing that breaks site rules. Past chat and purchase request records are kept for moderation and disputes; removal cannot be used to erase a pending transaction.
  11. Administrator oversight. Authorized moderators may review buyer-seller messages, attached listing images and purchase requests for fraud prevention, safety and dispute resolution. Users must not assume the chat is private from marketplace moderation.
  12. Moderation and reporting. Administrators can review listings, their images and conversations for fraud prevention and disputes. Listings can be removed or declined for safety or policy violations.
  13. Platform rules. Some publishers prohibit account transfers or resales. Buyers and sellers must comply with the relevant platform's terms and all applicable laws.
