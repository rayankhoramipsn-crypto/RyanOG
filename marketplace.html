TRISOCMD marketplace policy

These rules apply to every marketplace listing and conversation. Read them before contacting another user. This preview is not an active checkout or escrow platform.

  1. Legal ownership and transfer rights. Sellers may list only items they own and are permitted to transfer under relevant rules. Stolen, compromised or unauthorized accounts are forbidden.
  2. Accurate descriptions and real photos. Include the platform, condition, limitations and at least one genuine photo. Misleading listings, stock photos presented as the item, fake reviews and impersonation are prohibited.
  3. No fraud or manipulation. No scams, chargeback abuse, fake payment confirmations, pressure tactics, phishing links or requests for private recovery information.
  4. No secrets in messages. Never send passwords, session tokens, one-time codes, recovery phrases, private identification documents or other sensitive credentials.
  5. Verify independently. Approval is a moderation decision, not a guarantee of sale, account authenticity, seller reliability or compliance with a game's terms of service.
  6. Payments and escrow. The marketplace is in preview. TRISOCMD does not currently accept marketplace payments, hold funds, guarantee delivery or offer a functioning escrow service. Do not treat chat messages as payment instructions.
  7. Moderation and reporting. Administrators can review listings, their images and conversations for fraud prevention and disputes. Listings can be removed or declined for safety or policy violations.
  8. Platform rules. Some publishers prohibit account transfers or resales. Buyers and sellers must comply with the relevant platform's terms and all applicable laws.
