Store policies

Draft for review. These are not final legal terms and do not establish a live checkout or escrow service.

Product information

Product descriptions, availability, delivery method and limitations should be displayed before checkout. Customers should verify compatibility and intended use.

Payments and fulfillment

Checkout and payment processing are disabled in staging. Final payment, delivery, refund and dispute policies must be published before enabling purchases. Do not promise automatic refunds or guaranteed delivery until operationally supported.

Marketplace safety & administrator moderation

Only legitimate, authorized listings are permitted. Stolen accounts, compromised credentials, unauthorized access, fraud and prohibited content are not allowed. Sellers may withdraw their own listings and administrators may remove listings. To protect buyers and resolve disputes, listing withdrawal does not erase previous conversations or purchase requests. Authorized administrators can review buyer-seller conversations for fraud, dispute resolution and compliance with published rules.

Marketplace payments & seller payout policy (planned)

For future marketplace-managed sales, buyers would pay TRISOCMD through an officially published and verified payment method, never directly to sellers. Only after funds are independently verified, delivery is confirmed, and disputes are resolved would TRISOCMD arrange seller payout less the disclosed marketplace commission (currently proposed at 4%). Sellers must never solicit outside payment or pressure buyers to confirm delivery early.

Current limitation: the marketplace currently creates purchase requests only. TRISOCMD does not currently accept marketplace deposits, operate regulated escrow, verify payments automatically, process seller payouts, or guarantee refunds. Never transfer funds based on a chat message or a purchase request. Final terms, payment processor, compliance, refund and dispute procedures must be established before real payments are activated.

Privacy and account security

Never disclose your password, one-time codes or recovery links to sellers or support. The final privacy notice must explain data categories, retention, processors, user rights and contact details before public launch.

Contact and disputes

For questions or reports, contact the published support channel. A formal complaints and dispute process will be documented before production checkout is activated.

Review these drafts with applicable local consumer, privacy and e-commerce requirements before publishing.
