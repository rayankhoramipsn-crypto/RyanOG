3. One real workflow checklist

  1. Use separate browser profiles for buyer, seller and admin. Register/confirm a dedicated account, test recovery, then verify Remember Me on/off across browser restart.
  2. Seller: post a clearly tagged synthetic listing with a harmless PNG. It must remain pending and invisible to the buyer.
  3. Admin: inspect the private photo, approve the listing, then confirm buyer search/filter visibility.
  4. Buyer/seller: exchange a synthetic chat message; verify Realtime arrival, unread/read state and reconnect. A third account must not read that conversation or private unapproved media.
  5. Buyer: submit one purchase request; retry and confirm no duplicate request. Review buyer/seller order views and admin audit. Do not send funds; invoice creation must remain disabled and a TXID must never mark an order paid.
  6. Admin: publish a tagged synthetic blog article and add a harmless product image. Buyer/seller must not access content editing or staff support history. Test an order-linked synthetic support ticket.
  7. Check English, Persian and Arabic at 390px. Report only pass/fail and non-sensitive error codes. Preserve all historical records; do not remove unrelated data.
