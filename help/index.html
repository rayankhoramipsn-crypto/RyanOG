Frequently asked questions

Is checkout active?

No. Staging does not collect payments or process refunds or seller payouts.

How do I track an order?

Open Order tracking to view orders associated with your signed-in account.

How do I get support?

Sign in to create a ticket, view your ticket history, or message the support inbox.

What should I include in a ticket?

Describe the issue and include an order reference if relevant. Never send passwords, verification codes, or recovery links.
