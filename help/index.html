How can we help?

Guidance for accounts, shopping, marketplace and support. Checkout is not active in this staging preview.

Account

Sign in from the store's Account section. If you forgot your password, use its reset link and check your inbox and spam folder.

Orders

Before paying, verify the exact item, quantity, price and delivery terms. Never send passwords or recovery codes to anyone.

Marketplace

Listings require moderation. Keep conversations on the platform where supported, and report suspicious activity to support.

Frequently asked questions

Is checkout active?

No. The staging checkout is disabled pending end-to-end security and fulfillment checks.

Where can I ask for help?

Use the published Telegram support link. Never share your password, OTP, or authentication recovery code.

Can I publish an article?

Only authorized administrators can create or publish editorial content.

Can I add a video to a product?

Administrators can attach permitted YouTube review or unboxing links to product pages.

How do I report a problem?

Provide the page, product name, approximate time, and a description without including private credentials.
